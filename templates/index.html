About Me

Born in Brazil and raised between cultures, I bring a global perspective shaped by service in the U.S. Navy and over a decade working within Brazil’s judicial system. These experiences instilled discipline, accountability, leadership, and a deep respect for structure and compliance.

After earning a Law degree and serving as a Law Clerk Supervisor, I transitioned into IT to pursue my long-standing passion for technology. Over the past 3.5 years, I have grown from Help Desk to Cloud & Infrastructure roles, building hands-on experience in automation, patch compliance, virtualization, networking, and reliability engineering .

My goal is to become a Cloud & Security Engineer, designing resilient and secure cloud systems while embracing a location-independent lifestyle based in Europe. Whether working from my home office or a local coffee shop , I focus on building infrastructure that is scalable, observable, and secure. Outside of engineering, I recharge by exploring new roads on my motorcycle .
